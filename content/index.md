Howdy Aggies! 👋

Welcome to the ENGR4BBs handbook: a quick-reference resource hub built _by_ engineering students, _for_ engineering students tackling their first-year courses like ENGR/PHYS 216 and 217. Students find it useful to reference the handbook before they come to lab to grasp what they will be doing, and after lab writing their lab report to reinforce their understanding.

One thing to keep in mind: this handbook isn't meant to replace your official lab manual or course materials. Think of it as a friendly companion, a faster way to look things up, review concep ts, and see explanations from a fellow student's perspective. Always follow your instructor's materials first! 

New to engineering at Texas A&M? We recommend starting with [[Motivation and Mindset for Engineering]].

This notebook grows through collective effort, and we'd love your help! Whether it's fixing a typo, adding an explanation, or sharing a study tip that worked for you, every contribution makes this resource better for the next Aggie. Check out [[Contribute]] to see how you can get involved.
